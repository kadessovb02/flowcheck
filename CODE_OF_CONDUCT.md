# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on the work, and respect differences in experience and background. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly.

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and community spaces representing FlowCheck.
